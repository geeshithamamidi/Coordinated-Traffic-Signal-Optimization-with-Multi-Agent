# Scenarios

Generated by `python scenarios/generate_demand.py --scenarios single grid4x4`.
Counts are vehicles departing in the 3600 s episode (vehicles/hour).

- `surge`: medium demand plus an extra stream at twice the medium rate between 1200 s and 2100 s of the episode.
- `unbalanced`: medium demand with about 70% of trips starting on west-side edges (start node in the western quarter of the network) and the rest elsewhere.
